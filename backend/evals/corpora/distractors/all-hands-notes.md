# All-Hands Meeting Notes, Q2 2026

## People update

People Operations shared the results of the engagement survey. Scores for flexibility and remote work stayed high, while scores for career growth dropped slightly. The team is piloting a mentoring programme in the Lisbon office and will report back in September.

Several employees asked whether the vacation policy will change next year. No changes are planned; the leadership team will revisit the policy only after the annual benchmark study. A reminder was given to plan summer holidays early so that teams keep enough coverage.

There was a question about parental benefits for contractors. Contractors are engaged through their own agencies, so their benefits depend on the agency contract.

## Finance update

Revenue grew 18% quarter over quarter, mostly from the Growth plan of Ledgerline. Travel spending was above budget in April because of the customer summit in Toronto. Teams are asked to combine trips where possible and to book early, as late bookings were the main driver of the overspend.

Expense reports are taking longer to approve than last quarter. Finance is reviewing the approval chain and may introduce automatic approval for small amounts.

## Engineering update

The platform team migrated the search service to a new cluster. A minor incident during the migration caused elevated latency for about twenty minutes; it was handled by the on-call engineer and a postmortem was shared in the reliability meeting.

Deploy frequency increased to an average of eleven deploys per day. The team thanked everyone for keeping pull requests small. The question of moving to trunk-based development will be discussed in an RFC.

## Security update

The security team ran a phishing simulation in May. The click rate dropped compared to last year, and more people used the report button. Laptops that were missing updates were contacted directly. A new security training module about social engineering will be released next month.

## Product update

Customer Success shared that the most common support questions are about bank connections and invoice exports. A new onboarding checklist inside the product will launch in July. The integrations team is exploring additional payment processors, but nothing has been committed.

## Q&A

Someone asked about the office coffee machines in Nairobi; facilities will replace them in June. Another question was about a company offsite; the leadership team is looking at dates in the autumn but no location has been chosen.
