# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Version 4.2, effective March 1, 2026. Owned by People Operations.

## Working Hours and Location

Northwind Labs is a remote-first company. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone, Monday through Thursday. Fridays are reserved for focused work and no internal meetings may be scheduled on Fridays without a director's approval.

Employees may work from any country where Northwind has a legal entity: the United States, Canada, Germany, Portugal and Kenya. Working from another country for more than 30 consecutive days requires written approval from People Operations at least two weeks in advance, because it can create tax obligations for the company.

Each employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 1,200 USD in their first month. Co-working memberships are reimbursed up to 250 USD per month for employees who do not live within 40 km of a Northwind hub office. Hub offices are located in Toronto, Lisbon and Nairobi.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued monthly. Unused vacation can be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days; anything above 10 days expires on March 31 of the following year.

In addition to vacation, every employee gets 6 company-wide wellness days, announced at the start of each year. Sick leave is unlimited for illnesses lasting up to 5 consecutive working days; absences longer than that require a doctor's note sent to leave@northwindlabs.example.

Parental leave is 20 weeks at full pay for all parents regardless of gender, available within the first 18 months after a birth or adoption. Employees must have completed their probation period to be eligible.

Bereavement leave is 10 working days for an immediate family member and 3 working days for extended family.

## Probation and Performance Reviews

New hires have a probation period of 90 days. At day 45, the manager holds a mid-probation check-in. The probation outcome is confirmed in writing by day 90.

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in May and November. Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least three colleagues, and a manager assessment. Compensation adjustments are only made during the November cycle and take effect on January 1.

## Expenses and Travel

All expenses must be submitted in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt. Expenses submitted later than 60 days are not reimbursed.

Meals during business travel are covered up to a daily limit of 75 USD (or 70 EUR in Europe). Alcohol is never reimbursable. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; for flights of 6 hours or longer, premium economy may be booked. Business class requires approval from the CFO.

Hotels must be booked through Navan, the company travel tool, and the nightly rate must not exceed 220 USD in North America or 180 EUR in Europe unless the travel team has approved an exception.

Personal software subscriptions such as Grammarly or ChatGPT Plus are reimbursable up to 30 USD per month if your manager confirms they are used for work.

## Equipment

Engineers receive a 16-inch MacBook Pro; all other roles receive a 14-inch MacBook Air. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. A second monitor, keyboard and headset can be ordered through the IT portal at no cost to the employee.

When you leave Northwind, equipment must be returned within 14 days using the prepaid shipping box sent by IT. You may buy your laptop at its depreciated value if it is older than 2 years.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books, conferences and certifications. Unused learning budget does not roll over. Conference attendance that requires travel counts against the travel budget, not the learning budget, and must be approved by your manager 30 days before the event.

Employees can take 3 paid learning days per year to attend training or study for a certification.

## Code of Conduct

Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, with People Operations, or anonymously through the EthicsPoint hotline. Reports are investigated within 10 working days.

Gifts from vendors or customers worth more than 100 USD must be declared to Legal using the gift register form. Employees may not accept cash gifts of any amount.

## Benefits

In the United States, Northwind pays 100% of medical, dental and vision premiums for employees and 80% for dependents. The 401(k) plan has a company match of 4% of base salary, vesting immediately.

In Germany and Portugal, Northwind contributes to private supplementary health insurance and provides a monthly meal allowance. In Kenya, employees receive comprehensive private medical cover through AAR including outpatient care for the employee, spouse and up to four children.

The Employee Assistance Program offers six free confidential counselling sessions per year through Spring Health.
